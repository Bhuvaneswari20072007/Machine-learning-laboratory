STEP 6

Model Evaluation (Testing Dataset)

Evaluating regression accuracy on unseen 20% test samples (40 records).

MAE
Mean Absolute Error
--
Average magnitude of errors in marks.
$$\text{MAE} = \frac{1}{n}\sum |y_i - \hat{y}_i|$$
MSE
Mean Squared Error
--
Average squared difference between actual and predicted.
$$\text{MSE} = \frac{1}{n}\sum (y_i - \hat{y}_i)^2$$
RMSE
Root Mean Squared Error
--
Standard deviation of residuals in score marks.
$$\text{RMSE} = \sqrt{\text{MSE}}$$
R² Score
Coefficient of Determination
--
Proportion of variance explained by model.
$$R^2 = 1 - \frac{\text{SS}_{\text{res}}}{\text{SS}_{\text{tot}}}$$
